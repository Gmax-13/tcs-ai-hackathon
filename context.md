## Problem Statement

Hackathons provide students with an opportunity to solve real-world
problems, but teams may struggle to interpret the problem statement,
define the right scope, identify innovative features, and prepare an
effective final presentation. Build an AI-powered Hackathon Mentor
Assistant that acts as a virtual mentor throughout the hackathon
journey. The assistant should help teams understand the problem, apply a
design thinking approach, identify users and their needs, suggest unique
and practical features, recommend suitable technology options, and guide
students in preparing a clear prototype demonstration for the jury. The
assistant should support learning and decision-making without building
the complete solution on behalf of the team.

## Data Considerations

Use sample hackathon problem statements, evaluation criteria, design
thinking templates, sample solution outlines, technology options, and
presentation structures. Use synthetic examples or publicly available
material. Avoid collecting personal student performance data,
confidential competition information, or complete solutions from
previous teams.

**Example** A team receives a problem statement on reducing food wastage
in college canteens. The assistant explains the problem in simple
language, suggests stakeholder mapping, recommends demand prediction and
surplus donation features, and creates a step-by-step demo and jury
pitch checklist.

## Solution Expectations

Create a conversational assistant that accepts a hackathon problem
statement and limited team inputs. Generate a simple problem summary,
key questions, user personas, design thinking guidance, feature
suggestions, possible technology choices, prototype priorities,
validation checkpoints, and demo preparation tips. The output should
include a structured action plan, clear assumptions, innovation prompts,
and a concise jury presentation outline.

*Prototype target: simple conversational input, AI/rule-based mentoring
logic, structured recommendations, demo-preparation guidance, and
working demonstration.*

## 5 Non-Negotiables

1.  Understand the User
2.  Create Sample Data
3.  Agree Responsibilities & Contracts
4.  Build End-to-End
5.  Test Before Demo

## One Rule

**We are not judging documents. We are judging whether your solution
works and whether you can clearly explain it.**
